## How to submit

1. **Write the letter by hand** on clean white paper. Print this draft, then
   trace/write it in April's own handwriting. The 3 signatures must all be
   wet (real ink on paper). Do not sign digitally.
2. **Place her valid ID** next to the signed letter when photographing.
   Acceptable IDs: Philippine passport, or German Residence Card
   (Aufenthaltstitel).
3. **Take a clear photo** of the letter with the ID visible in the frame.
   Good lighting. All text and signatures must be readable.
4. **Send the photo to Ryan** so he can upload it to the MTA Portal under
   the **"Valid ID / Passport of Parents"** tab.

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Typing the letter — DSWD requires it handwritten.
- Only 1 or 2 signatures — must be exactly 3 wet signatures.
- Forgetting to include the flight details — travel arrangement is the
  main content DSWD looks for.
- No ID in the photo — DSWD uses the ID to verify the signer's identity.
- Signing with a stylus or digital signature — must be pen on paper.

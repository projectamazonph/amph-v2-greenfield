### Minor will Travel with a Companion
- Notarized Affidavit of Support is **not required**
- Submit one (1) latest proof of financial capability of the sponsor:
  - Certificate of Employment (COE)
  - Bank Statement
  - Income Tax Return (ITR)

### Minor will Travel Alone
- **Notarized Affidavit of Support** and proof of financial capability are **mandatory requirements**

### Supporting Documents
- Minor's Passport (Upload under the QR-Coded Birth Certificate tab)
- Consent to Travel (Upload under the valid ID/Passport of Parents tab)

### Important Reminders
- Minors below 13 years of age are not permitted to travel unaccompanied
- Airline assistance for minors below 13 years old is not allowed in our guidelines

### Allowed Applicant / Account Holder
- Legal Guardian (with Court Order)
- Parents or the designated travel companion
- For non-marital children (parents were not married before the minor's birth), only the mother may apply
- For marital children (parents were married before the minor's birth), either parent may apply

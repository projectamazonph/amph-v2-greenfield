## Step-by-step for April

1. **Print** `DSWD-Affidavit-of-Support-template.docx` on clean white paper (A4 or US Letter, both fine).
2. **Fill in by hand** every blank listed above under "Fill in with". Use BLACK INK, all caps for names, normal case for the rest.
3. **Sign** at the bottom where it says "Name and Signature of the Sponsor".
4. **Take the signed, filled-in form to a German authorized representative** (Notar / Notarin). Per Rana: the affidavit "must be notarized under authorized representative in the current residence of the sponsor." In Germany this is typically a Notar or a notarizing lawyer (Rechtsanwalt mit Notariat).
5. **Notary fills in** the SUBSCRIBED AND SWORN TO block, signs, and applies their seal/stamp.
6. **Scan** the fully notarized document. Color scan at 300 DPI minimum. Save as PDF.
7. **Send the PDF to Ryan** for upload to the MTA Portal.

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Forgetting to mark the `( ) alone / ( ) with companion` checkbox — DSWD needs to know Francesca is unaccompanied.
- Leaving the "purpose of travel" blank — DSWD requires the stated purpose.
- Signing before the notary — the notary must witness the signature, not the other way around.
- Submitting without notarization — the affidavit is **rejected** if not properly notarized in Germany.
- Filling in the SUBSCRIBED AND SWORN TO block — that is the notary's section, not April's.

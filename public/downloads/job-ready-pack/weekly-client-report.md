# Weekly Client Report

A one-page weekly update you send to a client every Monday. Same shape
every week, same numbers every week, same order every week. Clients who
see the same report shape every Monday stop wondering whether you
remembered to send it and start reading it.

Version: 2026-10-11. Pairs with Module 11 lesson 11.4 (Client
Communication Capstone) and Module 9 lesson 9.1 (Weekly Routine).

---

## How to use this template

- Send every Monday morning, before the client opens their laptop. Aim
  for the same hour each week; clients read reports on autopilot when
  the cadence is steady.
- Use the same numbers in every section. If the same number appears in
  two places, the report will not agree with itself by next week.
- Three sections: what changed, what the numbers say, what we are doing
  this week. Anything longer than a page tends to lose the client before
  they get to the third section.

## Worked example — Acme Co., week of 2026-10-06 to 2026-10-12

Subject: Acme PPC weekly — week ending 2026-10-12 (inbox)

### What changed this week

- Paused `SP-AUTO-WBOTTLE-32OZ-EXACT-MAN` ad group "leak proof case" after
  it crossed 60% ACoS over 14 days. Bid dropped from ₱18 to ₱12.
- Added 12 new negative exact terms from the STR (kids, school, glass,
  cheap, knock-off, off-brand, used, refurbished, broken, leaking,
  manual, replacement).
- Promoted "insulated bottle 32oz" from test ad group to main campaign
  after 14-day review ACoS came in at 24%.

### Numbers (vs. last week)

| Metric | This week | Last week | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spend | ₱18,420 | ₱19,100 | -4% |
| Sales | ₱84,650 | ₱78,920 | +7% |
| ACoS | 21.8% | 24.2% | -2.4 pts |
| TACoS | 8.4% | 9.1% | -0.7 pts |
| ROAS | 4.6x | 4.1x | +0.5x |
| CTR | 0.62% | 0.58% | +0.04 pts |
| CVR | 11.4% | 10.9% | +0.5 pts |
| Orders | 142 | 128 | +14 |

### What the numbers say

- ACoS dropped to 18-month low. Three drivers: STR-driven negative
  keyword pass, the bid cut on the bleeding ad group, and the
  "insulated bottle 32oz" promotion adding profitable new traffic.
- TACoS still above target 8% — will fall next week as last week's ad spend
  on now-paused groups stops counting.

### This week's plan

- Build the Sponsored Brands video variant for the top-performing
  Sponsored Products headline.
- Refresh the listing copy on B0CXYZ1234 — the bullet under "leak proof"
  is no longer accurate after last month's supplier change.
- Schedule the Q4 holiday push review for 2026-10-19.

### Open questions for the client

- None this week.

## Notes

- The eight metrics in the table are the only ones that go in the
  weekly. Adding more metrics dilutes the conversation; clients stop
  reading when there are ten rows of numbers they have to interpret.
- The "what the numbers say" section is two or three sentences in plain
  English. If you find yourself writing more than four sentences, split
  the analysis into a monthly deep-dive instead.
- The "this week's plan" section is three items, not five. The client
  needs to know what is changing on Monday, not your full backlog.
- This template is intentionally one page. If the report is running
  longer, you are over-explaining.

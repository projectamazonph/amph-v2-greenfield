▶M1How the Amazon Auction Actually Works4 MIN

Amazon runs an auction for every search, in milliseconds. Your bid is the most you are willing to pay per click — not what you actually pay. Amazon charges just enough to beat the advertiser below you (second-price auction), so a $1.00 bid often costs around $0.70.

Winning is not only about money. Ad placement = bid × relevance × expected CTR. A highly relevant listing with a great main image can beat a lazy listing bidding 2× more. This is why fixing your listing before scaling ads is not optional — it is math.

▶M2Match Types, With Live Examples5 MIN
BROAD

Fires on synonyms, related searches, variations in any order. Maximum discovery, minimum control. Your data-gathering net.

PHRASE

Fires when your phrase appears intact, with words allowed before or after. The middle ground between reach and control.

EXACT

Fires on your keyword and very close variants (plurals, minor misspellings). Maximum control. Where proven winners live.

🔬 INSTRUMENT: WILL MY AD FIRE? — YOUR KEYWORD IS "GARLIC PRESS"

The classic workflow: discover in Broad/Auto → find converting search terms → graduate them into Exact campaigns → negate the junk. This is called harvesting, and it is 80% of PPC skill.
▶M3ACOS, Break-Even, and Max CPC Math6 MIN

ACOS = Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue × 100. If you spend $10 to make $40 in sales, ACOS is 25%. The number to compare it against is your break-even ACOS — your profit margin before ads. ACOS below margin = profitable. Above = bleeding.

TACOS = Ad Spend ÷ TOTAL Revenue (ads + organic). It reveals whether ads are growing the whole business or just renting sales. Healthy listings trend TACOS downward over time as organic rank improves.

▶M4The Keyword Research Workflow5 MIN
  1. 01
    Seed. List 5–10 words a buyer would actually type. Your title, competitor titles, your brain. "garlic press", "garlic mincer", "garlic crusher".
  2. 02
    Expand. Add modifiers: material ("stainless steel"), audience ("for arthritic hands"), use case ("for ginger"), bundle ("set of 2"). Long-tail = cheaper clicks, higher intent.
  3. 03
    Score. Judge every keyword on three axes: volume (is there demand?), relevance (does the intent match my product?), competition (can my budget afford it?). Two of three is a maybe. One of three is a no.
  4. 04
    Structure. Group by intent. One ad group per tight theme. High-volume head terms get their own exact campaign so they cannot swallow the budget.
  5. 05
    Iterate. After 7–14 days, the search term report tells you the truth. Harvest winners to Exact. Negate losers. Repeat weekly.
Coach tip: relevance beats volume. 200 clicks from "cheap garlic press free shipping" converts worse than 8 clicks from "stainless garlic press heavy duty".
▶M5Negative Keywords: The Profit Lever4 MIN

Broad and phrase match will happily show your garlic press ad for "garlic powder" and charge you for the click. Negative keywords block specific search terms from triggering your ads. It is the single fastest way to cut ACOS without touching bids.

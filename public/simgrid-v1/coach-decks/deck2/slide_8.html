Module 4 · Lesson 4.4
Keyword Research Starter Process
Step 01 Write seed words

Identify the core product, material, use case, and size.

Step 02 Think like the shopper

Consider specific problems, gift recipients, or rooms.

Step 03 Prefer long-tail

More words mean clearer intent and less competition.

Step 04 Prioritize relevance

Relevance always comes first, then search volume.

Short-tail vs. Long-tail Economics

Type Example Volume Cost Intent
Short-tail "cutting board" Huge Expensive Vague
Long-tail "bamboo cutting board for vegetables" Small Cheap Clear buyer
Coach tip: A small budget should buy only clear intent. "Cutting board" burns budget on browsers. "Large bamboo cutting board for kitchen" finds buyers.

The Listing Does the Selling
The First Law of PPC
Ads rent attention, listings convert it. A weak page turns ad budget into tuition. You pay for clicks that never had a chance.
Worked Example
100 clicks at $1.00 = $100 spend. One sale of $25.
Before touching bids, ask: would a stranger buy from this page?
How Shoppers Judge
  • Shoppers compare you with 15 other results in seconds.
  • Reviews are borrowed trust.
  • Price is judged relative to neighbors, not in absolute terms.
Coach tip: Fix the page first. Then advertise. Always in that order.

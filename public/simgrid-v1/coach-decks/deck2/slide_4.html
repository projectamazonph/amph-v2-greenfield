The Three Ad Types
Sponsored Products
Shows as
Single product in search and product pages
Best for
Core sales — start here
Sponsored Brands
Shows as
Brand banner with logo and several products
Best for
Brand-registered sellers, defense
Sponsored Display
Shows as
On and off Amazon, retargeting
Best for
Remarketing and awareness
  • Beginners: 90% of learning happens in Sponsored Products.
  • Add Brands and Display only after Sponsored Products is stable.
Coach tip: Master one ad type deeply before touching the others.

Negative Keywords
A negative keyword tells Amazon: never show my ad for this. Stop paying for shoppers who can never buy.
  • 01 Find terms with clicks but zero sales
  • 02 Confirm they are truly irrelevant
  • 03 Add as negative (phrase or exact)
  • 04 Log the change and move on
Worked Example
A bamboo board getting clicks for "plastic cutting board" is paying to show the wrong product to the wrong shopper.
Action: Negate "plastic"
Coach tip: Negatives are the fastest profit lever in PPC. Five minutes a week saves real money.

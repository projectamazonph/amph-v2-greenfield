Diagnosing Conversion Leaks
Low CTR + High Conv
Weak hook, strong page
Fix: Better main image and title to attract more clicks.
High CTR + High Conv
It works
Fix: Scale carefully. Increase budget or bids slightly.
Low CTR + Low Conv
Wrong targeting or offer
Fix: Refine keywords and improve the page content.
High CTR + Low Conv
Page or price issue
Fix: Improve reviews, price, or A+ content. Do not raise bids.

Common Mistakes

  • Raising bids to fix a listing problem.
  • Adding more keywords to a page that cannot convert.
  • Copying a competitor's price without having their review count.
Coach tip: The symptom table is your stethoscope. Use symptoms to find the leak before prescribing fixes. Diagnose, then prescribe.

Budget Basics
Budget is the daily cap. Bid is what you offer per click. Together they set how many clicks you can afford.
Lower CPC = More Clicks
$10 Budget ÷ $0.50 CPC = 20 Clicks
Higher CPC = Fewer Clicks
$10 Budget ÷ $1.00 CPC = 10 Clicks
Pacing Scenarios
Budget Avg CPC Max clicks/day
$10 $0.50 20
$10 $1.00 10
$20 $1.00 20
$50 $1.25 40
Coach tip: Budget gone by noon? You are invisible all evening — often the best shopping hours. A profitable campaign out of budget is a shop closing at noon.

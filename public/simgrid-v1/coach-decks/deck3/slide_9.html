Module 8 · Lesson 8.2
Winners and Wasters
Pattern Verdict Action
Sales + ACOS under margin Winner Promote to Exact
Sales + ACOS over margin Expensive winner Lower bid
No sales, many clicks, irrelevant Waster Negative
No sales, few clicks Too early Watch
No sales, many clicks, relevant Leak Fix page or price
Do
  • Decide with 7+ days of data.
  • Negate irrelevant terms fast.
  • Promote winners into Exact.
Don't
  • Negate after only 2 clicks.
  • Pause a campaign for one bad term.
  • Let wasters run "just in case".
Coach tip: Every week, money flows to wasters unless you redirect it. Match each pattern to its safe action.

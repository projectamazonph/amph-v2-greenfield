New Product Launch Plan
The 4-Week Timeline
Week Focus Actions
1 Learn Auto only, small bids, watch terms
2 Control Exact campaign with long-tail winners
3 Clean First negatives pass
4 Scale +10–20% budget on proven winners only
Worked Example: $20/day Budget
Week 1: All $20 to Auto.

Week 2: $8 Auto + $12 Exact.

Week 4: Shift more budget to what converts.
Launching is not spending fast. It is learning fast with small money.
Coach tip: Week 1's goal is data, not profit. Follow a plan that learns before it scales.

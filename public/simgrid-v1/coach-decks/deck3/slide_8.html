Module 8 · Lesson 8.1
Reading the Search Term Report
The 4-Step Reading Method
Step 01

Download the report for the last 7–14 days

Step 02

Sort by spend, highest first

Step 03

Read clicks, sales and ACOS per term

Step 04

Mark each term: winner, waster or maybe

Column Dictionary

Column Question it answers
Search term What did the shopper type?
Clicks How much attention did it buy?
Spend What did it cost?
Sales What came back?
ACOS Was the trade worth it?
Coach tip: Start at the top of the spend column. That is where your money is deciding.

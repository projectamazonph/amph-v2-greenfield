Bid Rules for Beginners
Situation Reading Safe Action
No impressions Too low or irrelevant Raise a little, check relevance
Clicks, no sales Wrong terms or weak page Negatives + listing check
Sales + low ACOS Winning Raise bid a little, watch
Sales + high ACOS Too expensive Lower bid
Budget dies early Winning but capped Raise budget if profitable
Common Mistakes
  • Bidding high on everything just to "get data".
  • Cutting bids on a proven winner because of one quiet day.
  • Never revisiting bids after the initial launch.
Coach Tip

Bids are dials, not switches. Make small turns, then wait for the data to settle.

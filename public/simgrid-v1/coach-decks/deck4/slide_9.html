The Permissions Ladder
Level 3: Stop & Ask
Strategy & Budget
Requires manager approval before execution.
Level 2: Do & Notify
Structural Changes
Execute the task, but flag it in the weekly report.
Level 1: Independent
Routine Optimization
Execute silently as part of the standard SOP.

Task Mapping

Task Permission Level
Adding negative keywords Level 1
Micro bid adjustments (±10%) Level 1
Launching new exact campaigns Level 2
Pausing a core campaign Level 3
Increasing budget by >20% Level 3
Coach tip: Trust is earned through transparency. Never guess on a Level 3 task. A good VA knows exactly where their authority ends.

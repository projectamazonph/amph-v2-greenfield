Module 10 · Lesson 10.2
Explaining Numbers in Human Words
Clients do not care about acronyms. They care about profit, growth, and waste. Translate the data into business decisions.
Instead of saying (Jargon) Say this (Business Value)
"ACOS dropped from 45% to 30% this week." "We cut wasted spend on irrelevant searches. The campaigns are now breaking even and ready to scale."
"CTR is 0.15% on our main exact campaign." "Shoppers see our ad but aren't clicking. We need to test a new main image or check our price against competitors."
"Conversion rate fell to 4%." "People are clicking but not buying. Let's check if we lost the Buy Box or got a negative review."
"We are capping out our daily budget." "Our ads turn off before the evening rush. We should raise the budget by $10 to capture those profitable sales."
Coach tip: Speak in dollars and decisions. Your job is not to read the spreadsheet to the client; your job is to tell them what the spreadsheet means.

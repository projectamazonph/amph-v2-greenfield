SOPs and the Change Log

Standard Operating Procedures

Stop guessing every week. Turn judgment into a repeatable, documented process. If X happens, do Y.

Trigger (The "If")
ACOS > 40% AND Clicks > 20
Action (The "Then")
Decrease bid by 15%

The Change Log

Memory lies, logs do not. When performance suddenly drops, the log tells you exactly what broke it.

Date Target Change Reason
Oct 12 [bamboo board] Bid $1.00 → $0.85 ACOS was 45%
Oct 12 "plastic board" Added Negative 15 clicks, 0 sales
Oct 19 Auto Campaign Budget $20 → $25 Capping out at 2pm
Coach tip: If you didn't log it, it didn't happen. The change log is your only defense when a client asks why sales dropped on a Tuesday.

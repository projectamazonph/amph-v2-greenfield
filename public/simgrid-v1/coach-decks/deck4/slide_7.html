Troubleshooting Top of Funnel
No Impressions
Symptom
0 Impressions, 0 Clicks
Diagnosis (Why it happens)
  • Bid is too low to win the auction.
  • Campaign is out of budget.
  • Listing does not have the Buy Box.
The Fix

Check Buy Box and inventory first. If healthy, raise the bid slightly and wait 48 hours.

Low Click-Through Rate
Symptom
High Impressions, CTR < 0.3%
Diagnosis (Why it happens)
  • Main image is weak or blends in.
  • Price is uncompetitive vs. neighbors.
  • Review count/rating is too low.
The Fix

Improve the listing hook (image, price, coupon). Do not raise bids — you will just buy more ignores.

Coach tip: Troubleshoot in order. If they don't see it, they can't click it. If they don't click it, they can't buy it. Fix the top of the funnel first.

How Much Data is Enough?
The Click-Confidence Ladder
1–5 Clicks
Noise. Do nothing.
10–15 Clicks
Emerging pattern. Watch closely.
20+ Clicks (0 Sales)
Clear failure. Negate or lower bid.

Premature Optimization

Reacting to 2 or 3 clicks is reacting to randomness.

A normal conversion rate is 10%. That means 9 out of 10 clicks will not buy.

If you pause a keyword after 3 clicks, you kill potential winners before the math has a chance to work.

Coach tip: Patience saves profit. Wait for statistical significance (usually 15–20 clicks) before declaring a keyword a waster.

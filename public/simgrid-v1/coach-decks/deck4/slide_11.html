The Final Exam
To graduate to independent management, you must complete a full account audit and present a 30-day strategy:
  • Identify the top 3 wasted spend leaks.
  • Propose a new campaign structure for the top seller.
  • Calculate the break-even ACOS and target margins.
  • Present the plan as a client-facing narrative report.
Coach tip: The capstone proves you can make decisions, not just click buttons. It is the bridge from student to professional.

Troubleshooting Bottom of Funnel
Clicks, No Sales
Diagnosis

Shoppers are interested enough to click, but the page or offer fails to convince them.

The Fix

Check your price against competitors. Improve reviews and listing quality. Add negative keywords if the traffic is irrelevant.

High ACOS
Diagnosis

You are getting sales, but you are paying too much for each one relative to your margin.

The Fix

Lower bids on expensive, broad terms. Shift budget toward high-converting, long-tail exact match keywords.

Sudden Sales Drop
Diagnosis

A previously stable campaign suddenly stops producing orders.

The Fix

Check external factors first: Did you lose the Buy Box? Are you out of stock? Did a recent bid cut kill your visibility?

Coach tip: Bottom-of-funnel problems are usually listing problems or bid problems. Do not try to fix a conversion leak by adding more keywords.

Simple Report Structure
1

The Headline

Spend, sales, and ACOS summary.

2

The "Why"

What drove the change in performance.

3

The Action

What we did about it this week.

4

The Next Step

What we are watching for next week.

5

The Ask

Do we need anything from the client?

Data Dump (Forces client to do the math)

"Spend was $500. Sales were $2000. ACOS is 25%. Impressions went up 10%. Clicks were 500. CPC is $1.00."

Narrative Report (Tells the story)

"We spent $500 to make $2,000 at a profitable 25% ACOS. [1] Sales grew because our new exact match campaign for 'bamboo board' found traction. [2] We increased its budget by 15% to scale. [3] Next week, we will monitor if the ACOS holds steady at the new budget. [4] No action needed from you. [5]"

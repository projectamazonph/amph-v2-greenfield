Module 9 · Lesson 9.2
One Change at a Time
Do
  • Change one variable at a time
  • Wait about a week for data to settle
  • Compare before and after metrics
  • Log the change and the reason
Don't
  • Make 20 bid changes on a Monday
  • Add keywords + change bids + increase budget on the same day
  • Judge performance on overnight data
The Confounded Change Problem

You raise bids and add new keywords on the same day. Sales jump. Which change worked? Nobody knows — and that is the problem.

Coach tip: Science beats vibes. One variable, one verdict.

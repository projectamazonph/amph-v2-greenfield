Every stage leaks. Your job is to find the biggest leak and fix that one first.
Diagnosing Funnel Leaks
Stage What can go wrong
Impressions Bid too low, targeting too narrow
Clicks Weak main image, price, or title
Page Views Out of stock, slow page load
Orders Bad reviews, high price, weak bullets
Coach tip: Read the funnel top to bottom: impressions, CTR, conversion, ACOS. Do not change many things at once, or nothing can be learned.

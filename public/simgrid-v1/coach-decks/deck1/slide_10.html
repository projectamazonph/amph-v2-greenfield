ACOS & Break-even

Advertising Cost of Sales (ACOS)

ACOS = Spend ÷ Sales × 100

Worked Example

If you spend $20 on ads and generate $100 in sales:

$20 ÷ $100 × 100 = 20% ACOS

For every dollar of ad sales, 20 cents went to paying for ads.

ACOS vs 30% Profit Margin
ACOS Reading Action
15% Profitable Room to scale
30% Break-even Ads pay for themselves
45% Losing money Fix bids or targeting
No sales Emergency Check relevance & listing
Coach tip: Never say "good ACOS" without asking: good compared to which margin? Break-even ACOS equals your profit margin. Below it is profit; above it is loss.

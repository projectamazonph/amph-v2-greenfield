Pay Per Click in Plain Words
Impression
The ad was shown to a shopper. This is free.
Click
A shopper clicked your ad. This is what you pay for.
Bid
The maximum amount you are willing to pay for one click.
Cost Per Click (CPC)
CPC = Spend ÷ Clicks
The average price you actually paid for one click.
Worked example: You spend $20 on 20 clicks, so your average CPC is $1.00 per click. If 2 of those clicks buy your $25 product, those clicks produced $50 of sales.
Coach tip: Clicks cost money whether they buy or not. That is why targeting and listing quality decide profit.

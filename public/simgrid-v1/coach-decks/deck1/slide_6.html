The Auction: Bid + Relevance

The Split-Second Auction

Every search starts a silent auction. Sellers bid for the chance to show, and Amazon scores how relevant your product is to that exact search.

  • A high bid with a bad match loses to a fair bid with a great match.
  • Relevance = keyword fit + listing quality + history.
  • You never pay more than your bid.
One search, one auction
01 Shopper types a search
02 Interested sellers enter the auction
03 Amazon weighs bid + relevance
04 Winning ads get placements
05 Seller pays only if clicked
Worked example: You sell bamboo boards. For the search "bamboo cutting board" you are highly relevant. For "knife set" you are irrelevant — no bid can save you.
Coach tip: Relevance is free leverage. Tight keywords and a good listing lower what you pay over time.

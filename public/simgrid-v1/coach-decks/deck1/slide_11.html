ROAS, TACoS & Full Worked Example
Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
ROAS = Sales ÷ Spend
The mirror of ACOS. Higher is better.
Total ACOS (TACoS)
TACoS = Ad Spend ÷ Total Revenue
Ads compared to the whole business, including organic sales.
Metric Use it when...
ACOS Judging individual campaigns
ROAS Quick comparisons
TACoS Checking whether ads grow the whole brand
Worked Example: Keyword Economics
Product: Bamboo board ($25)
Profit after costs: $7.50
Profit Margin (Break-even ACOS): 30%
Scenario CPC Conversion Cost per sale ACOS Verdict
Broad keyword $1.00 10% $10.00 40% Loses money
Long-tail keyword $0.60 15% $4.00 16% Profitable
Same product, same page — different keyword economics. That is why choosing keywords is money management.
Coach tip: When ACOS is too high, do not only cut bids. A more specific keyword is often the cheaper fix.

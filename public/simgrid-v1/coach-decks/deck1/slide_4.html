Organic vs Paid and the Buy Box

Visibility Types

Type Meaning How you get it
Organic Free result Earned by relevance, sales and reviews
Paid (Sponsored) Ad result You pay per click
The Buy Box Constraint: The Buy Box is where the "Add to Cart" button lives. Most sales go through it. If you don't win it, your ads can send shoppers to a competitor.
Why use ads? Appear faster than waiting for organic rank, discover which search words sell, protect your page from competitor ads, and grow sales velocity.

The Visibility Loop

  • Shopper searches
  • Results page: organic + sponsored
  • Shopper clicks
  • Landing page convinces (or not)
  • Buy or leave
Coach tip: Ads buy visibility. The listing earns the sale. Both must work.

The listing is the conversion engine

What the shopper judges

Shoppers compare you with many results in seconds. Ads bring the visit; the page must remove doubt.

Coach tip: When a campaign performs badly, inspect the listing before blaming the ads.
Listing partWhy it changes ad performance
Main imageFirst visual hook in search and on the page
TitleClarifies product and relevance
PriceCompared instantly with neighbors
BulletsAnswer “what is in it for me?”
Reviews + ratingBorrowed trust; reduces doubt
Description / A+Explains details and objections
Stock + Buy BoxNo stock or no Buy Box wastes paid traffic
Worked example: Two identical cutting boards can produce different ACOS when one has a sharp image, 4.7 stars, and a credible price while the other does not.

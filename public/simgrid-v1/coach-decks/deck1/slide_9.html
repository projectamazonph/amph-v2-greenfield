CTR and Conversion Rate
The two rates that tell you where the leak is:
Click-Through Rate (CTR)
CTR = Clicks ÷ Impressions
Of the people who saw the ad, how many clicked? Measures the strength of your hook.
Conversion Rate
Conv. Rate = Orders ÷ Clicks
Of the people who clicked, how many bought? Measures the strength of your page and targeting.
CTR Reading Likely Meaning
Under 0.3% Image, price, or title not appealing
0.3% – 0.7% Decent hook
Over 0.7% Strong hook

Worked Example

1,000 impressions and 5 clicks is a 0.5% CTR.
5 clicks and 1 order is a 20% conversion.

Story: Decent hook, good page.

Coach tip: Low CTR means fix the hook (image, price, title). Low conversion means fix the page or the targeting.

Spend, Sales and CPC
Metric Plain meaning Formula
Spend Money the ads consumed Total cost
Sales Revenue the ads produced Attributed sales
CPC Average price of one click Spend ÷ Clicks
Coach tip: Spend alone tells you nothing. Always compare spend against sales and clicks to understand performance.

Cost Per Click (CPC)

CPC = Spend ÷ Clicks

Worked Example

If your campaign has $30 spend, generates $120 in sales, and receives 30 clicks:

$30 ÷ 30 clicks = $1.00 per click

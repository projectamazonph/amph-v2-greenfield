# Checklist — Before You Report

Run this Friday afternoon before the report goes out — to a manager,
to a client, or to your own future self. A clean report makes a
manager nod, not yawn.

## Numbers

- [ ] **Spend** in the right currency (₱ or $), matching the account.
- [ ] **Sales** are _ad-attributed_ sales unless I clearly labeled them
      as total. Don't mix the two.
- [ ] **ACOS** shown to one decimal place (e.g. 23.4%, not 23%).
- [ ] **ROAS** shown as a ratio (e.g. 4.3×, not 430%).
- [ ] **TACoS** uses _total_ sales, not ad sales.
- [ ] **CTR / CVR / CPC** included if the manager expects them.
- [ ] **No double-counted campaigns** (portfolios vs campaigns).

## Trend

- [ ] Every KPI has a **trend vs last week** — one line, plus or minus X%.
- [ ] The trend is honest. If sales are up 8% but profit is down 4%,
      I say both, not just the one that flatters me.
- [ ] I have looked at the trend, not just the snapshot. A 12% ACOS
      with a sharp upward trend is more urgent than a 25% ACOS that
      is flat.

## What worked

- [ ] **1–2 examples**, each with the number that proves it.
- [ ] I named the _target_ (campaign, ad group, keyword) — not just
      "the account did well."
- [ ] I explained _why_ it worked, in one sentence, so the next VA
      can repeat it.

## What did not work

- [ ] **Honest, not blamey.** "The phrase match bled ₱840 on
      11 clicks with 0 sales" beats "things were tough."
- [ ] I named the target and the metric that failed.
- [ ] I noted whether I already fixed it, or whether the fix is
      in next-week's plan.

## Next steps

- [ ] **2–3 bullets**, each with an owner by name.
- [ ] Each bullet is actioned in the next 7 days, not "soon."
- [ ] Each bullet has a number attached — expected lift, expected
      ACOS, expected spend.

## Risks & asks

- [ ] **Risks** called out: stock, Buy Box, competitor moves, big
      spend outpacing budget.
- [ ] **Asks from the owner** are real and need a decision — not
      padding. If I have no asks, I say so.

## Housekeeping

- [ ] **Date range** clear in the title. Currency, account name,
      and ASIN in the header.
- [ ] **Prepared by** my name. **Reviewed by** my lead if it is a
      client report.
- [ ] **Change log attached** as an appendix if the report goes to
      a manager or client.
- [ ] **Proofread.** No typos in the numbers. No "the the." No
      leftover last-week's data in the charts.

## One-line summary

> Numbers, trend, what worked, what didn't, next steps, owner.
> Six things. Every Friday. In that order.

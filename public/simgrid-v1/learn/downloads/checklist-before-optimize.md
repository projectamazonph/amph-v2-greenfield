# Checklist — Before You Optimize

Run this every Monday before you touch any bid, target, or budget.
If any box is empty, fix it first. "Optimize around a problem" is the
most expensive mistake in PPC.

## The boring four (always first)

- [ ] **Stock** — every ASIN in scope is in stock for the next 7+ days.
- [ ] **Price** — no recent price change on me or my competitors.
- [ ] **Reviews** — no new 1- or 2-star review cluster in the last 7 days.
- [ ] **Competitor** — no new entrant or aggressive price move this week.

If any of the four is broken, **stop optimizing the campaign** and
report up. The right fix is upstream, not in the bid.

## Data hygiene

- [ ] Reports pulled for the **last 7 days** (or 14 if 7 is too thin).
- [ ] Each target I plan to touch has **at least 14 clicks** since
      the last change. Below that, I'm reading noise.
- [ ] I am not editing any campaign that is **less than 7 days old**,
      unless it is obviously broken (paused, rejected, wrong product).

## The change of the week

- [ ] I have picked **one** change for this week.
- [ ] I have written the change in the log **before** I made it:
      what, why, expected effect, when I'll re-check.
- [ ] The change is reversible. I know how to undo it in 60 seconds.
- [ ] I am not stacking multiple changes on the same campaign this week.

## Search term harvest

- [ ] Search term report pulled, last 7 days, sorted by spend desc.
- [ ] Walked the top 20 by spend. Marked each: winner, waster, maybe.
- [ ] **Winners** (sales + ACOS under margin) ready to promote to Exact
      at a safe bid.
- [ ] **Wasters** (5+ clicks, 0 sales, clearly irrelevant) ready to
      negative-exact.
- [ ] **Maybes** (under 10 clicks) left alone. Watch next week.

## Bid & budget review

- [ ] Walked every active campaign. Flagged any with: - Spend = budget by 2pm (missed-sale flag) - ACOS > 2× margin for 7 days (stop-loss candidate) - 0 impressions for 48+ hours (paused or underbid)
- [ ] Considered placement adjustments **only** on campaigns with
      14+ days of data and proven winners.
- [ ] Considered a budget raise **only** on a campaign that has
      already hit its current cap for 5+ days in a row.

## End of session

- [ ] Change log updated. Every edit dated, named, justified.
- [ ] Screenshot of the dashboard saved if the week had a notable
      change.
- [ ] Next-week plan written in 3 bullets, even if the answer is
      "no changes, hold course."

## One-line summary

> One change at a time. Wait a week. Log it. Repeat.

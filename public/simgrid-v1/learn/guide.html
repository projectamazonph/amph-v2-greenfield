Student guide

From zero to operating VA

A six-week path through all 12 modules, 60 lessons, 12 module quizzes, and the final exam. About 35 hours of study and practice. Take it at your own pace — but finish each week's exit check before moving on.

🖨️ Press Ctrl+P to save this page as PDF

The two rules that matter most

  1. One change at a time. Science beats vibes. Change one variable, wait a week, compare before-and-after, log it.
  2. Stock, price, reviews, competitors — in that order. When something breaks, check the boring four before touching any campaign setting.

After· On the job Ongoing
Goal

Operate, don't panic.

What to do
  • Follow the daily / weekly / monthly cadence from the VA Cadence handout.
  • Use the Permissions Ladder from M12 — small safe changes alone, big levers with approval.
  • Write every bid change in a change log. Memory lies; logs don't.
  • When in doubt: stock, price, reviews, competitors — in that order.
  • Re-skim the three operating checklists once a month. They are the muscle-memory you want.
Exit check: You are operating under supervision. After 30 days of clean change logs, you earn unsupervised work.

What to do when you're stuck

  1. Re-read the relevant module lesson. Most answers are in there.
  2. Search the glossary for the unfamiliar word.
  3. Ask the AI Coach in plain words. Try the suggested questions first.
  4. Run the relevant practice tool (Triage, Campaign Architect, Audit, Pacing) — the act of doing it surfaces the gap.
  5. Take the relevant module quiz. The wrong answers tell you exactly which lessons to revisit.
  6. Only then: ask a teacher or mentor. Bring your notes and your best guess.

Every Formula You'll Need

One sheet, every metric. Print this and pin it.

Spend · Sales · ACOS · ROAS · TACoS · CTR · CVR · CPC
Eight numbers. Eight formulas. That's it.
MetricFormulaWhat it tells youHealthy range
ACOSSpend ÷ Sales × 100% of sales eaten by ad spendUnder your profit margin
ROASSales ÷ Spend₱ returned per ₱1 spentOver 1 ÷ margin (e.g. margin 30% → ROAS > 3.33)
TACoSAd spend ÷ Total sales × 100Ad share of total revenueTrends down over time as organic grows
Break-even ACOSProfit margin %The line where you neither win nor loseAim well below it
CTRClicks ÷ Impressions × 100Ad creative + relevance0.3–0.5%+ for most niches
CVROrders ÷ Clicks × 100Listing quality + price match10%+ for healthy products
CPCSpend ÷ ClicksHow competitive the auction isCompare to your break-even CVR
ImpressionsFrom reportReach — are you showing up?Trended, not absolute

Rule of thumb: if a number is moving the wrong way, walk it up the diagnostic order — delivery → engagement (CTR) → conversion (CVR) → economics (ACOS/ROAS) — before you change anything.

Checklist · Before You Launch

Run this the day before a new campaign goes live.

  1. Listing is retail-ready: title, bullets, A+ content, 7+ images, price competitive, 15+ reviews (or a plan to get them).
  2. Buy Box is yours at 100% for the last 14 days, or you have a clear fallback.
  3. Inventory covers the test window — at least 8 weeks of stock at expected daily sales.
  4. Campaign naming follows the standard (ProductLine · Targeting · MatchType · Goal).
  5. One match type per ad group — no Exact and Broad mixed in the same group.
  6. Budgets are isolated per campaign — no shared daily caps that starve the test.
  7. Bids start at or below the suggested bid for new campaigns; you can raise after data, not before.
  8. Negatives seeded with the obvious irrelevants (free, used, knockoff, repair, manual, alternative).
  9. Goals written down — what does success look like in 14 / 28 days?
  10. Change log started — first entry: "Launch, all defaults, no edits yet."

Checklist · Before You Optimize

Run this every Monday before touching any bid or target.

  1. Boring four checked: stock, price, reviews, competitor. Don't optimize around a problem the listing created.
  2. Data window is long enough — at least 14 clicks per target before judging performance.
  3. One change at a time this week. Write it in the change log before you make it.
  4. Search term report pulled for the last 7 days, sorted by spend desc.
  5. Winners promoted to Exact at safe bids; wasters negated exact or phrase.
  6. Bid cuts only on targets with 14+ clicks — never on small samples.
  7. Budget reviews done — any campaign dying by 2pm is a missed-sale flag.
  8. Placement adjustments evaluated — Top-of-Search +50% only on proven winners.
  9. No edits to campaigns < 7 days old unless they are obviously broken (paused, rejected, wrong product).
  10. End-of-session: change log updated, screenshot saved, next-week plan written.

Checklist · Before You Report

Run this Friday afternoon before the report goes out.

  1. Numbers in the right units: spend in ₱ or $, sales attributed correctly, ACOS to one decimal.
  2. Trend vs last week on each KPI — one line, plus or minus X%.
  3. What worked — 1–2 examples, with the number that proves it.
  4. What did not work — honest, not blamey. The number that shows it.
  5. Next steps — 2–3 bullets, actioned in the next 7 days, each owned by name.
  6. Risks called out — stock, Buy Box, competitor moves, big spend outpacing budget.
  7. Asks from the owner — only if you actually need a decision from them.
  8. Date range clear in the title. Currency, account name, and ASIN in the header.
  9. Prepared by your name. Reviewed by your lead if it's a client report.
  10. Change log attached as an appendix if the report goes to a manager or client.

ACOS Cheat Sheet

One page. Every ACOS question you'll be asked.

ACOS = Spend ÷ Sales × 100
Lower is better. Always compare to your margin.
ACOSReadingAction
Under marginProfitableRaise bid a little, scale carefully
At marginBreak-evenWatch; small bid cuts are fine
Over marginLosing moneyCut bid, tighten match, or negate
No salesEmergencyCheck relevance, listing, Buy Box

Break-even ACOS = your profit margin. A 30% margin means 30% ACOS is the break-even line. Anything below 30% likely makes money; anything above likely loses money. This is the single most important fact about ACOS.

Match Types Cheat Sheet

Broad, phrase, exact — and when to use each.

TypeReachControlUse it for
BroadHighLowResearch. Discover new search terms.
PhraseMediumMediumMid-funnel. Words must include your phrase.
ExactLowHighControl and scale. Use for proven winners.

Rule of thumb: Start tight (Exact). Use Broad and Auto only as scouts. Promote research winners into Exact. Never run a new account on Broad only.

Negative keywords block irrelevant searches. They are the fastest profit lever in PPC. Add wasters from the search term report weekly.

Search Term Mining Cheat Sheet

The weekly harvest, in seven steps.

  1. Download the search term report for the last 7–14 days.
  2. Sort by spend, highest first.
  3. Mark each term: winner, waster, or maybe.
  4. Winners (sales + ACOS under margin): promote to Exact.
  5. Wasters (high clicks, no sales, irrelevant): add as negative.
  6. Maybes (under 10 clicks): watch — too little data.
  7. Log every change. Repeat next week.

Weekly Report Format

What every report needs, in order.

  1. Numbers: spend, sales, ACOS, ROAS, clicks, orders, CTR, conversion.
  2. Trend vs last week. One line, plus or minus X%.
  3. What worked. One short paragraph, 1–2 examples.
  4. What did not work. One short paragraph, honest.
  5. Next steps. 2–3 bullets, actioned in the next 7 days.
  6. Prepared by. Your name, date.

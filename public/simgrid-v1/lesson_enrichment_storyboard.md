# Module Deck Enrichment Storyboard

Every module retains its twelve-slide sequence: **orientation, seven concept lessons, decision practice, safety check, evidence note, and assessment gate**. Each concept lesson keeps the plain-language definition and adds one structured visual aid. Every module includes a clickable decision exercise whose answer is also stated in the debrief, so interaction never hides essential content.

## Slide-level interaction rule

The exercise slide will show a short scenario and three decision choices. Selecting a choice gives immediate plain-language feedback; the correct answer is also visible in the exercise debrief. The evidence-note slide turns the same decision into a small table so learners practice recording a reason, owner, and review date.

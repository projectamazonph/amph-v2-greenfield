    The VA Playbook

    Repeatable routines, decision trees and the formulas your account lead expects you to know cold.

    1. Open Campaign Manager, set the date range to Yesterday. Scan spend vs. daily budget on every campaign.
    2. Budget pacing: any campaign that ran out before ~2pm? Raise its budget 20% or shift budget from campaigns that underspend.
    3. Sort keywords by Spend. Open the top 10: ACOS > 1.3× target → lower bid 10–15%. ACOS < 0.5× target AND low impressions → raise bid 10–20%. Never move a bid more than 20% at once.
    4. Download the Search Term Report (last 7 days). Two passes: (a) irrelevant terms with clicks → add as negative exact; (b) terms with 2+ orders in broad/phrase → harvest into exact.
    5. Log every change in the client change log: date · entity · old → new · reason. A VA who logs is a VA who stays.

    Escalate immediately if you see: ACOS >100% for 3 days · CTR halved overnight (possible suppression or lost Buy Box) · $0 spend (policy block or budget bug).

    • Index check first: search B0XXXXXXXX + keyword. No appearance = not indexed = fix the listing before spending a cent.
    • Title formula: Brand + Core Keyword + Material/Attribute + Size/Count + Use Case + Differentiator. First 80 chars = the mobile title. ≤200 chars total.
    • Bullets: all 5 filled, 100–250 chars, BENEFIT — proof structure, secondary keywords woven in naturally.
    • Backend: 249 bytes, spaces only, no repeats of title words. Add misspellings + Spanish + synonyms. Never competitor brands.
    • Main image: pure white (RGB 255,255,255), product fills ≥85% of the frame.
    • Price sanity: within ~15% of the top 5 competitors, or your conversion rate bleeds no matter how good the ads are.
    ACOS ABOVE TARGET? ├ Clicks < 15 → not enough data. Wait 72h. ├ CVR below category avg → listing problem (price, images, reviews) — don't touch the bid. ├ CVR fine, CPC high → lower bid 10–15% or tighten to exact. └ CVR fine, CPC fine, few sales → relevance mismatch → negatives or pause. ACOS LOW + impressions low? └ You're leaving money. Raise bids 10–20%, add broad. TACoS rising while ACOS is flat? └ Organic rank is slipping. Escalate to the account lead.

    Pull the search-term report, filter clicks ≥ 5 and 0 orders, then read intent. Classic negatives:

    • Intent mismatch: free, used, repair, parts, manual, pdf, cheap
    • Competitor brand names
    • Wrong material: glass, plastic on a bamboo product
    • Wrong audience: kids on an adult product

    Add each as negative exact and keep a client-level shared negative list so the same junk never leaks across campaigns.

    Auto → Broad → Phrase → Exact. Reach shrinks, control grows as you move right. Rule of thumb:

    • Exact = proven converters with their own bid.
    • Phrase = controlled discovery.
    • Broad = the net that catches new winners.
    • Auto = wildcard for product/category discovery.

    Harvesting is the weekly loop: when a search term converts inside broad/auto, lift it into its own exact-match campaign and bid to it specifically. That's how a campaign matures from "spending" to "scaling".

    WEEKLY PPC REPORT — [Client] — [Dates] SPEND $___ | SALES $___ | ACOS __% (target __%) | ORDERS __ WINS: [top keyword, harvests, new exact winners] ISSUES: [budget caps, ACOS offenders, suppressed ASINs] CHANGES: [entity | old -> new | reason] NEXT WK: [1-3 priorities] ESCALATE: [anything blocking — Buy Box, stock, policy]
    • Week 1 — Discovery: one Auto campaign + 2–3 Broad campaigns on your best long-tail keywords. Bids ~110% of suggested. Budget sized to guarantee ≥15 clicks/day per campaign so you get data, not guesses.
    • Week 2 — Prune: first search-term sweep. Kill irrelevant Auto targets and add your first negatives. Don't harvest yet — too early.
    • Week 3 — Harvest: every search term with 2+ orders at or below target ACOS moves into its own Exact campaign with its own bid. Lower the Broad bid on harvested terms so they stop competing with themselves.
    • Week 4 — Scale: raise winners 10–20%, cut losers, test a +30–50% top-of-search placement if CVR supports it, then lock the weekly cadence from SOP 01.

    Launch rules: never judge a keyword before 15 clicks; log every change; keep TACoS in view — launch ACOS is allowed to run above break-even on purpose, because you are buying rank and velocity, not just today's profit.

    Placements are where your ad lands. Top of search = first row above organic results → highest CTR and highest CPC. Product pages = cheaper, lower-intent browsing traffic. A placement multiplier stacks on the bid: a $1.00 bid with +50% top-of-search can pay up to $1.50 for that slot. Add the multiplier only when that placement's CVR earns it.

    1. Account Health → Quality Alerts / policy notifications. Read the exact violation — don't guess.
    2. Common culprits: title over 200 chars, ALL-CAPS words, promotional phrases ("Best Seller", "Free Shipping"), restricted words, or a main image with text/badges.
    3. Fix at the root via the flat file (bulk upload template) — cleaner and more reliable than the Seller Central UI for style fixes.
    4. Wait 15 minutes to 24 hours for reindexing; verify with the ASIN + keyword search test.
    5. Still suppressed? Open a case with the alert ID + before/after screenshots attached.
    6. Log the incident so the same offending claim never ships in a future listing again.
    RESTOCK ETA vs DAYS OF STOCK? ├ Stock > gap + 7 days → run campaigns as normal. ├ Stock ≈ gap → taper: lower bids/budget 10–15% every 2–3 days. ├ Stock < gap → stretch: small price lift + cut bids; plan a restock-day relaunch. └ Already OOS? → pause ads (don't burn impressions on a dead page), then prep relaunch: RESTOCK DAY └ Re-enable at pre-OOS bids, add a coupon, expect 3–7 days to regain rank. Never restart at 2× the old bid "to catch up" — that buys expensive, low-quality clicks.

    Dayparting = shifting spend toward the hours and days that convert best (via bid rules or third-party tools — Sponsored Products has no native hour-level scheduler). It earns its keep only with 100+ clicks/week per campaign and clear CVR peaks (e.g. B2B products on weekday mornings). Never daypart a fresh campaign — you need the data first.

    Seasonality raises CPCs across the board in Q4; Prime Day week brings a CPC spike and a hangover the week after. Pre-load budget for December, and don't panic-trim bids the Monday after a deal event — read the search-term report first.

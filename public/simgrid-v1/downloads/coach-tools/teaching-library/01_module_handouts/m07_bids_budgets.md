# Module 7 Field Handout — Bids & Budgets

## The job

By the end of this module, you should be able to **Set budget and bid logic, diagnose pacing, and make controlled bid changes.** The required practice handoff is **Bid Decisions + Pacing Deck** (`bid-decisions.html / pacing-deck.html`), with a target of **75% each / 70% quiz**.

> **Operating rule:** Change one variable, define the guardrail, and write the review date before you click save.

## What to look for

- **1.** Bid versus target ACOS
- **2.** Budget exhaustion timing
- **3.** Clicks and orders after a change
- **4.** Evidence volume and review window

## Worked thinking

The course example reduces a bid from $0.90 to $0.65 while preserving $50 sales and cutting spend from $27 to $14. The lesson is not “always lower bids”; it is to test a justified change against a guardrail.

## Practice before you open the simulator

Write a bid-change record with baseline, reason, proposed action, expected signal, guardrail, owner, and review date.

## Decision standard

Raise only when incremental traffic is relevant and economics support it; lower or hold when the evidence does not.

## Key language

| Term     | Plain-language meaning                                            |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Bid      | Maximum willingness to compete for an eligible click opportunity. |
| Budget   | Spend capacity over a defined time period.                        |
| Pacing   | How budget is consumed across the day or period.                  |
| Baseline | The measured condition before a controlled change.                |

## Exit ticket

Write one sentence for each prompt before moving on: What is the observed evidence? What is your smallest safe action? What guardrail or review date will tell you whether the action worked?

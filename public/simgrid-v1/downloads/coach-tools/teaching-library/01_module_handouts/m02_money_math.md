# Module 2 Field Handout — Money Math

## The job

By the end of this module, you should be able to **Calculate CPC, CTR, conversion rate, ACOS, ROAS, TACoS, and break-even economics.** The required practice handoff is **Keyword Lab** (`keyword-lab.html`), with a target of **75% simulator / 70% quiz**.

> **Operating rule:** Use the metric that identifies the leak; do not optimize a ratio without its denominator.

## What to look for

- **1.** Impressions and clicks for visibility and attention
- **2.** Orders and clicks for conversion rate
- **3.** Spend and sales for ACOS / ROAS
- **4.** Contribution margin for break-even ACOS

## Worked thinking

The course example of $20 spend on $100 sales produces 20% ACOS. If contribution margin is 30%, a 20% ACOS leaves a 10-point contribution buffer before other costs.

## Practice before you open the simulator

Calculate $1 CPC from $30 spend / 30 clicks, 0.5% CTR from 5 clicks / 1,000 impressions, 20% CVR from 1 order / 5 clicks, and 20% ACOS from $20 / $100 sales.

## Decision standard

Compare ACOS to the break-even or target guardrail before raising, holding, or lowering a bid.

## Key language

| Term  | Plain-language meaning                   |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------- |
| CTR   | Clicks divided by impressions.           |
| CPC   | Spend divided by clicks.                 |
| CVR   | Orders divided by clicks.                |
| ACOS  | Ad spend divided by ad-attributed sales. |
| ROAS  | Ad-attributed sales divided by ad spend. |
| TACoS | Ad spend divided by total sales.         |

## Exit ticket

Write one sentence for each prompt before moving on: What is the observed evidence? What is your smallest safe action? What guardrail or review date will tell you whether the action worked?

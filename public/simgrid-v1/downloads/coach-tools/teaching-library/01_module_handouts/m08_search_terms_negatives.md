# Module 8 Field Handout — Search Terms & Negatives

## The job

By the end of this module, you should be able to **Read reports, classify winners, wasters, and maybes, and run the weekly harvest loop.** The required practice handoff is **Search Term Triage + Bulk File** (`search-triage.html / bulk-file.html`), with a target of **75% each / 70% quiz**.

> **Operating rule:** Harvest is a classification workflow: graduate, negate, lower / repair, or watch.

## What to look for

- **1.** Relevance to the product
- **2.** Clicks and orders
- **3.** ACOS versus the guardrail
- **4.** Existing campaign coverage and negative status

## Worked thinking

A relevant winner with profitable evidence can move to Exact. Irrelevant high-click traffic should become a negative. Low-volume terms deserve a watch state rather than a forced verdict.

## Practice before you open the simulator

Run a harvest table with ten terms. Write the decision, evidence, destination campaign or negative level, and the next review date.

## Decision standard

Make the smallest action that protects learning while controlling waste.

## Key language

| Term    | Plain-language meaning                                               |
| ------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Harvest | Recurring review of search-term evidence for actionable discoveries. |
| Winner  | Relevant traffic meeting the economic and evidence rule.             |
| Waster  | Irrelevant or economically unacceptable traffic requiring control.   |
| Watch   | Insufficient evidence to change safely.                              |

## Exit ticket

Write one sentence for each prompt before moving on: What is the observed evidence? What is your smallest safe action? What guardrail or review date will tell you whether the action worked?

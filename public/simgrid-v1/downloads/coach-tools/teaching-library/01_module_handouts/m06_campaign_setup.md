# Module 6 Field Handout — Campaign Setup

## The job

By the end of this module, you should be able to **Choose auto versus manual, build a safe starter structure, and plan a four-week launch.** The required practice handoff is **Campaign Architect + AdConsole Pro** (`campaign-architect.html / ad-console.html`), with a target of **75% each / 70% quiz**.

> **Operating rule:** Launch with a learning plan, a budget guardrail, and a review date—not a pile of unowned targets.

## What to look for

- **1.** Listing readiness gate
- **2.** Campaign purpose and naming
- **3.** Auto / manual role
- **4.** Week-by-week evidence milestones

## Worked thinking

A starter structure separates discovery from control. Auto or broader discovery gathers evidence; exact or controlled campaigns protect proven terms.

## Practice before you open the simulator

Complete a one-product launch brief with campaign names, budgets, targets, an evidence hypothesis, and Day 7 / 14 / 28 review questions.

## Decision standard

Open only the campaigns you can explain, monitor, and change according to a named review cadence.

## Key language

| Term              | Plain-language meaning                                            |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Auto campaign     | Amazon-directed discovery targeting.                              |
| Manual campaign   | Advertiser-selected targeting and match settings.                 |
| Launch hypothesis | A testable expectation about where early evidence will come from. |
| Guardrail         | A limit that prevents uncontrolled spend or risk.                 |

## Exit ticket

Write one sentence for each prompt before moving on: What is the observed evidence? What is your smallest safe action? What guardrail or review date will tell you whether the action worked?

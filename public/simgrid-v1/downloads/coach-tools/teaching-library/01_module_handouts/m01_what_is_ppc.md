# Module 1 Field Handout — What is PPC?

## The job

By the end of this module, you should be able to **Explain PPC, auction logic, relevance, placements, and the click funnel.** The required practice handoff is **AdConsole Pro** (`ad-console.html`), with a target of **75% simulator / 70% quiz**.

> **Operating rule:** An ad is a route into a funnel, not a guarantee of a sale.

## What to look for

- **1.** Search intent or shopper context
- **2.** Bid competitiveness
- **3.** Relevance of keyword to product
- **4.** Listing conversion after the click

## Worked thinking

A click is only an entry into the listing. Diagnose whether the failure sits before the click, on the product page, or in the offer.

## Practice before you open the simulator

Map a shopper from query to impression, click, listing, cart, and order. Label one signal that could break at each step.

## Decision standard

Use bids to compete for a relevant opportunity; use listing work to earn the order.

## Key language

| Term       | Plain-language meaning                                                  |
| ---------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| PPC        | Pay-per-click advertising; cost is incurred when an ad is clicked.      |
| Auction    | The selection process that weighs bid and relevance for an opportunity. |
| Placement  | Where an ad can appear, such as search or product pages.                |
| Conversion | A desired outcome, commonly an order after a click.                     |

## Exit ticket

Write one sentence for each prompt before moving on: What is the observed evidence? What is your smallest safe action? What guardrail or review date will tell you whether the action worked?

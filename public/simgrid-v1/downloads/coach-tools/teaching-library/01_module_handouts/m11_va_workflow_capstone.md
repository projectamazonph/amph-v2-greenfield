# Module 11 Field Handout — VA Workflow & Capstone

## The job

By the end of this module, you should be able to **Operate by cadence, respect permissions, use SOPs and change logs, and complete the capstone.** The required practice handoff is **Client Onboarding + Capstone Sequence** (`client-onboarding.html / capstone-sequence.html`), with a target of **75% onboarding / 85% capstone / 70% quiz**.

> **Operating rule:** A reliable operator knows what to do, what to log, what to notify, and what must be approved.

## What to look for

- **1.** Task owner and due date
- **2.** Permission level
- **3.** Evidence artifact
- **4.** Escalation status and client communication

## Worked thinking

A correct action without a record cannot be reviewed or taught. A change log and permission rule turn an individual action into an accountable operating system.

## Practice before you open the simulator

Build a capstone case file that connects a business objective, diagnosis, selected change, permission level, expected evidence, and final narrative.

## Decision standard

Act independently only within the permission level; notify or escalate when authority or risk requires it.

## Key language

| Term              | Plain-language meaning                                                |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SOP               | Standard operating procedure; repeatable step sequence with controls. |
| Independent       | Action allowed without separate approval.                             |
| Notify            | Action allowed but must be reported to the owner.                     |
| Approval-required | Action cannot proceed until an authorized owner approves.             |
| Capstone          | Integrated performance task that demonstrates operational judgment.   |

## Exit ticket

Write one sentence for each prompt before moving on: What is the observed evidence? What is your smallest safe action? What guardrail or review date will tell you whether the action worked?

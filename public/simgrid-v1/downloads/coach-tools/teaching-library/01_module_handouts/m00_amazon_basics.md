# Module 0 Field Handout — Amazon Basics

## The job

By the end of this module, you should be able to **Explain marketplace visibility, listing anatomy, organic versus paid traffic, and the Buy Box.** The required practice handoff is **BuyBox Dojo** (`listing.html`), with a target of **75% simulator / 70% quiz**.

> **Operating rule:** Before touching traffic, make sure the product can win the click and the conversion.

## What to look for

- **1.** In-stock status and buyable offer
- **2.** Price and coupon position
- **3.** Review proof and image clarity
- **4.** Search relevance and Buy Box ownership

## Worked thinking

If a shopper cannot buy the offer, additional impressions cannot create a sale. Start the diagnosis with availability, not advertising.

## Practice before you open the simulator

Audit one listing for stock, price, reviews, images, title relevance, and Buy Box readiness. Write the first conversion leak you would repair.

## Decision standard

Repair listing or offer friction before proposing a traffic increase.

## Key language

| Term            | Plain-language meaning                                           |
| --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ASIN            | Amazon Standard Identification Number for the product record.    |
| Buy Box         | The preferred offer position that enables most purchase actions. |
| Organic traffic | Visits earned without an ad click.                               |
| Paid traffic    | Visits generated after an ad click.                              |

## Exit ticket

Write one sentence for each prompt before moving on: What is the observed evidence? What is your smallest safe action? What guardrail or review date will tell you whether the action worked?

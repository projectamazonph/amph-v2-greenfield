# Module 5 Field Handout — Listing Readiness

## The job

By the end of this module, you should be able to **Diagnose listing conversion leaks before increasing PPC pressure.** The required practice handoff is **BuyBox Dojo** (`listing.html`), with a target of **75% simulator / 70% quiz**.

> **Operating rule:** Do not scale traffic into a conversion leak.

## What to look for

- **1.** Stock and Buy Box status
- **2.** Price competitiveness
- **3.** Review count and rating signal
- **4.** Main image, title, bullets, and offer clarity

## Worked thinking

When clicks arrive but orders do not, check stock, price, reviews, and competitors before assuming a bidding problem.

## Practice before you open the simulator

Score a listing as ready, repair first, or escalate. Identify the highest-impact defect and the owner who can correct it.

## Decision standard

Hold or limit traffic if the listing has an unresolved conversion-critical defect.

## Key language

| Term            | Plain-language meaning                                                    |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Readiness gate  | A condition that must be met before a launch or scale decision.           |
| Conversion leak | A product-page or offer problem that prevents orders after clicks.        |
| Offer           | The sellable combination of price, availability, and fulfillment.         |
| Escalation      | A documented request for an owner with the authority to resolve an issue. |

## Exit ticket

Write one sentence for each prompt before moving on: What is the observed evidence? What is your smallest safe action? What guardrail or review date will tell you whether the action worked?

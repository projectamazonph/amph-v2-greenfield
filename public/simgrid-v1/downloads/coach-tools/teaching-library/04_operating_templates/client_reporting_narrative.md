# Client Reporting Narrative Template

## Use

Use this in Module 10 to turn data into a decision-ready story. Avoid metric dumps.

## Executive update

**Result:** [State the result versus baseline or target.]  
**Driver:** [State the most credible evidence-backed explanation.]  
**Action:** [State what was changed or what is proposed.]  
**Guardrail:** [State what must not worsen and how it will be watched.]  
**Checkpoint:** [State the next review date or decision condition.]

## Classification guardrails

| Classification | Use when                                                      | Action                                                    |
| -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Graduate       | Relevant and economically acceptable with sufficient evidence | Add to controlled Exact or chosen destination.            |
| Negative       | Materially irrelevant or established waste                    | Block at the right campaign or ad-group level.            |
| Lower / repair | Relevant but above margin or structurally flawed              | Lower bid, repair listing / targeting, then set a review. |
| Watch          | Evidence is insufficient                                      | Do not force a verdict; collect more signal.              |

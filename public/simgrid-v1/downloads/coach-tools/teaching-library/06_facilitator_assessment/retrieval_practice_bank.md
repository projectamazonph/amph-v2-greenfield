# PPC Coach Retrieval Practice Bank

## How to use

Use these prompts at the beginning or end of a lesson. Require a one- or two-sentence answer with evidence or a formula where relevant.

| Module | Retrieval prompt                                              | What a strong answer includes                     |
| -----: | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- |
|      0 | Why can more traffic fail to produce more sales?              | Buyability, offer, and listing conversion logic.  |
|      1 | What happens between a shopper query and an order?            | Funnel sequence and a possible break point.       |
|      2 | What does ACOS measure, and what must it be compared against? | Spend / sales and margin or target guardrail.     |
|      3 | Why separate campaign purposes?                               | Control, readability, and cleaner actions.        |
|      4 | What is the difference between a keyword and search term?     | Instruction versus shopper evidence.              |
|      5 | What is the troubleshooting order before PPC?                 | Stock, price, reviews, competitors, PPC.          |
|      6 | What must every launch plan include?                          | Purpose, guardrail, owner, evidence, review date. |
|      7 | What makes a bid change controlled?                           | Baseline, one variable, guardrail, review date.   |
|      8 | Name the four harvest classifications.                        | Graduate, Negative, Lower / repair, Watch.        |
|      9 | Why change one variable at a time?                            | Preserves causal learning.                        |
|     10 | What turns a metric update into a useful report?              | Result, driver, action, guardrail, checkpoint.    |
|     11 | When should a VA escalate?                                    | Permission or risk outside agreed authority.      |

# Four-Week Launch Operating Guide

## Purpose

Use this guide for Module 6. It turns a campaign launch into a learning sequence instead of a one-time setup task.

| Window     | Primary question                            | Evidence to collect                              | Typical safe decision                        |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------- |
| Days 1–7   | Is relevant traffic entering the structure? | Impressions, clicks, query relevance, budget use | Confirm setup or repair obvious structure.   |
| Days 8–14  | Which terms show credible intent?           | Search terms, CTR, early orders, listing signal  | Classify early evidence; avoid overreacting. |
| Days 15–21 | What can be controlled or blocked?          | Term-level spend, sales, ACOS versus guardrail   | Graduate, negate, lower / repair, or watch.  |
| Days 22–28 | What deserves more or less investment?      | Controlled-change result and pacing              | Scale or hold only with evidence.            |

## Operating rule

Use a separate record for each change. A target does not “need optimization”; it needs a diagnosis, a hypothesis, one variable, a guardrail, and a review date.

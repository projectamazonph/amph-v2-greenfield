# Listing-to-Launch Implementation Guide

## Purpose

Use this guide to connect Modules 0, 5, and 6. The sequence prevents the common error of trying to solve a listing or offer problem with more traffic.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the product is buyable and the offer is in stock.
2. Review price, coupon, reviews, image clarity, title relevance, and competitor pressure.
3. Mark the listing Ready, Repair first, or Escalate in the Listing Readiness Audit.
4. If Ready, write a launch brief that names the objective, budget guardrail, campaign purpose, and Day 7 review question.
5. If Repair first or Escalate, document the owner and hold the scale decision until a defined gate is cleared.

## Failure mode to avoid

Do not call a low conversion rate “a bid problem” before the readiness checks are complete.

## Evidence artifact

Submit the Listing Readiness Audit and Four-Week Launch Brief with a named owner and review date.

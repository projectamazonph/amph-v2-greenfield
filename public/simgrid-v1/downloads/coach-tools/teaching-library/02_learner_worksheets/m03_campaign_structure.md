# Module 3 Learner Worksheet — Campaign Structure

## Objective

Use this worksheet while completing **Campaign Architect**. Its job is to make your reasoning visible. Do not write an action without first writing the evidence and the guardrail.

> **Rule to apply:** Structure exists to preserve control, reading clarity, and a clean next action.

## Part B — Worked transfer

A campaign that mixes discovery, harvesting, and defense hides the cause of performance. Separate the purpose before changing the bid.

**Your transfer task:** Draft a campaign tree for one product: discovery, phrase/exact control, and product-targeting. Explain what data would move a term between groups.

## Part D — Explain it back

1. What would make you _not_ take the action you selected?
2. What competing explanation should you rule out first?
3. What evidence would cause you to escalate rather than act independently?

## Completion check

I can state the signal, the diagnosis, the action, the guardrail, and the review checkpoint without relying on a vague phrase such as “optimize” or “improve.”

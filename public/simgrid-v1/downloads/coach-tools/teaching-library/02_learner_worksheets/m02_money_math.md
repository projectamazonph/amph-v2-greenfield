# Module 2 Learner Worksheet — Money Math

## Objective

Use this worksheet while completing **Keyword Lab**. Its job is to make your reasoning visible. Do not write an action without first writing the evidence and the guardrail.

> **Rule to apply:** Use the metric that identifies the leak; do not optimize a ratio without its denominator.

## Part B — Worked transfer

The course example of $20 spend on $100 sales produces 20% ACOS. If contribution margin is 30%, a 20% ACOS leaves a 10-point contribution buffer before other costs.

**Your transfer task:** Calculate $1 CPC from $30 spend / 30 clicks, 0.5% CTR from 5 clicks / 1,000 impressions, 20% CVR from 1 order / 5 clicks, and 20% ACOS from $20 / $100 sales.

## Part D — Explain it back

1. What would make you _not_ take the action you selected?
2. What competing explanation should you rule out first?
3. What evidence would cause you to escalate rather than act independently?

## Completion check

I can state the signal, the diagnosis, the action, the guardrail, and the review checkpoint without relying on a vague phrase such as “optimize” or “improve.”

# Module 8 Learner Worksheet — Search Terms & Negatives

## Objective

Use this worksheet while completing **Search Term Triage + Bulk File**. Its job is to make your reasoning visible. Do not write an action without first writing the evidence and the guardrail.

> **Rule to apply:** Harvest is a classification workflow: graduate, negate, lower / repair, or watch.

## Part B — Worked transfer

A relevant winner with profitable evidence can move to Exact. Irrelevant high-click traffic should become a negative. Low-volume terms deserve a watch state rather than a forced verdict.

**Your transfer task:** Run a harvest table with ten terms. Write the decision, evidence, destination campaign or negative level, and the next review date.

## Part D — Explain it back

1. What would make you _not_ take the action you selected?
2. What competing explanation should you rule out first?
3. What evidence would cause you to escalate rather than act independently?

## Completion check

I can state the signal, the diagnosis, the action, the guardrail, and the review checkpoint without relying on a vague phrase such as “optimize” or “improve.”

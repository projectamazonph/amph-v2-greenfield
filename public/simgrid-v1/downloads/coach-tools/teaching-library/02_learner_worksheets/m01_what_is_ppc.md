# Module 1 Learner Worksheet — What is PPC?

## Objective

Use this worksheet while completing **AdConsole Pro**. Its job is to make your reasoning visible. Do not write an action without first writing the evidence and the guardrail.

> **Rule to apply:** An ad is a route into a funnel, not a guarantee of a sale.

## Part A — Evidence scan

## Part B — Worked transfer

A click is only an entry into the listing. Diagnose whether the failure sits before the click, on the product page, or in the offer.

**Your transfer task:** Map a shopper from query to impression, click, listing, cart, and order. Label one signal that could break at each step.

## Part C — Decision record

| Decision record field      | Your response | Coach check                                             |
| -------------------------- | ------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Observed condition         |               | What did the simulator, listing, report, or brief show? |
| Business or shopper impact |               | Why does this matter before the next action?            |
| Evidence threshold         |               | What minimum evidence makes a decision defensible?      |
| Decision                   |               | Choose the smallest safe action.                        |
| Guardrail                  |               | What must not get worse?                                |
| Owner / permission         |               | Who can execute, notify, or approve?                    |
| Review date                |               | When will you check the result?                         |

## Part D — Explain it back

1. What would make you _not_ take the action you selected?
2. What competing explanation should you rule out first?
3. What evidence would cause you to escalate rather than act independently?

## Completion check

I can state the signal, the diagnosis, the action, the guardrail, and the review checkpoint without relying on a vague phrase such as “optimize” or “improve.”

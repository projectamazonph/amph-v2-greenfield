# Module 4 Learner Worksheet — Keywords & Match Types

## Objective

Use this worksheet while completing **Keyword Lab + Search Term Triage**. Its job is to make your reasoning visible. Do not write an action without first writing the evidence and the guardrail.

> **Rule to apply:** A keyword is your instruction; a search term is the shopper evidence you must classify.

## Part B — Worked transfer

A profitable, relevant search term may graduate to Exact. An irrelevant high-click search term should be blocked with a negative, not rescued with a lower bid.

**Your transfer task:** Classify six terms as graduate, lower / repair, negative, or watch. Write the evidence that justifies each classification.

## Part D — Explain it back

1. What would make you _not_ take the action you selected?
2. What competing explanation should you rule out first?
3. What evidence would cause you to escalate rather than act independently?

## Completion check

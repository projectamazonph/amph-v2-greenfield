# Module 6 Learner Worksheet — Campaign Setup

## Objective

Use this worksheet while completing **Campaign Architect + AdConsole Pro**. Its job is to make your reasoning visible. Do not write an action without first writing the evidence and the guardrail.

> **Rule to apply:** Launch with a learning plan, a budget guardrail, and a review date—not a pile of unowned targets.

## Part B — Worked transfer

A starter structure separates discovery from control. Auto or broader discovery gathers evidence; exact or controlled campaigns protect proven terms.

**Your transfer task:** Complete a one-product launch brief with campaign names, budgets, targets, an evidence hypothesis, and Day 7 / 14 / 28 review questions.

## Part C — Decision record

## Part D — Explain it back

1. What would make you _not_ take the action you selected?
2. What competing explanation should you rule out first?
3. What evidence would cause you to escalate rather than act independently?

## Completion check

I can state the signal, the diagnosis, the action, the guardrail, and the review checkpoint without relying on a vague phrase such as “optimize” or “improve.”

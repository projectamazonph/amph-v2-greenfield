# Module 7 Learner Worksheet — Bids & Budgets

## Objective

Use this worksheet while completing **Bid Decisions + Pacing Deck**. Its job is to make your reasoning visible. Do not write an action without first writing the evidence and the guardrail.

> **Rule to apply:** Change one variable, define the guardrail, and write the review date before you click save.

## Part B — Worked transfer

The course example reduces a bid from $0.90 to $0.65 while preserving $50 sales and cutting spend from $27 to $14. The lesson is not “always lower bids”; it is to test a justified change against a guardrail.

**Your transfer task:** Write a bid-change record with baseline, reason, proposed action, expected signal, guardrail, owner, and review date.

## Part D — Explain it back

1. What would make you _not_ take the action you selected?
2. What competing explanation should you rule out first?
3. What evidence would cause you to escalate rather than act independently?

## Completion check

I can state the signal, the diagnosis, the action, the guardrail, and the review checkpoint without relying on a vague phrase such as “optimize” or “improve.”

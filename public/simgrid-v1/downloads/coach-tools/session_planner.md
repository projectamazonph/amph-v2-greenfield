## Class sequence

| Segment                 |   Time | Asset                               | Instructor move                                          | Learner output                 |
| ----------------------- | -----: | ----------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Retrieval               |  5 min | Scenario card or assessment item    | Ask for the prior rule or formula without notes.         | Individual answer              |
| Visual explanation      | 10 min | Framework diagram or deck slide     | Connect the visual to one decision rule.                 | Plain-language restatement     |
| Simulator demonstration | 15 min | Relevant simulator                  | Think aloud: evidence → inference → action → guardrail.  | Annotated workflow             |
| Paired practice         | 20 min | Learner worksheet and scenario card | Require one defended decision per pair.                  | Simulator result and rationale |
| Debrief                 | 10 min | Change log or report template       | Compare evidence and ask what would change the decision. | Logged decision                |
| Exit ticket             |  5 min | Assessment bank                     | Collect a transfer response before progression.          | Short written response         |

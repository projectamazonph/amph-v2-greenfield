## Permission reminder

**Level 1 — Independent:** routine optimization that follows an approved SOP. **Level 2 — Do and notify:** structural work that should be documented in the weekly report. **Level 3 — Stop and ask:** strategy, core-campaign, or material-budget decisions requiring approval.

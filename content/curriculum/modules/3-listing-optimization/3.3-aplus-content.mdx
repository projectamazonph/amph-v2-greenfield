## What you can do after this lesson

After this lesson, you can tell a client whether Brand Registry and A+ Content are worth pursuing. You can also estimate the ACoS improvement enrolling might produce.

## The decision in one sentence

A+ Content tends to raise conversion rate, and a higher conversion rate tends to lower ACoS at the same bid. That is the lever. Confirm Brand Registry eligibility first, then validate the lift with your own data. Treat it as a hypothesis, not a guarantee.

If you're running Amazon PPC without Brand Registry, you're fighting with one hand tied behind your back. Brand Registry unlocks A+ Content (enhanced product descriptions with rich images and comparison charts) that directly improves your ad performance.

## How A+ Content Boosts PPC Performance

### The Conversion Rate Lift

Amazon's own marketing describes potential sales lifts of up to 8% for Basic A+ Content and up to 20% for well-implemented Premium A+ Content. Separately, aggregated seller-reported conversion-rate lifts are often cited in the **5-17%** range across categories. Treat both as directional, not a guarantee for any specific listing. Even a conservative lift matters a lot for PPC, using a ₱38 CPC (cost per click) for this example:

| Scenario | Conversion Rate | ACoS at ₱38 CPC |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|
| No A+ Content | 6% | 50% |
| Basic A+ Content | 8% | 38% |
| Premium A+ Content | 10% | 30% |

**Same product. Same ad. Same bid. Better listing = 20 percentage points lower ACoS.**

### Why Conversion Rate Is Everything in PPC

```
Higher Conversion Rate → Stronger relevance signal → Lower CPC
                                                    ↓
                                       MORE clicks for same budget
                                                    ↓
                                       Even MORE sales data
                                                    ↓
                                       Even STRONGER relevance signal
```

A+ Content kicks off this virtuous cycle by improving the thing that matters most for ad efficiency: **does this listing convert?**

The lift depends on the listing and category, and Amazon doesn't publish a guaranteed conversion-rate figure. Treat any cited percentage as a directional range, not a promised outcome for your specific product.

## A+ Content Modules That Work Best for PPC

Not all A+ modules are created equal. Here are the ones that most impact conversion:

### Module 1: Comparison Chart (Highest Impact)

Shows your product side-by-side with other products in your line. This is the #1 highest-converting A+ module.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│              COMPARISON CHART: OUR BAMBOO RANGE              │
├──────────────┬──────────┬──────────┬──────────┬─────────────┤
│              │ Standard │  Large   │   XL     │  Premium    │
│              │  Board   │  Board   │  Board   │  Set (3pc)  │
├──────────────┼──────────┼──────────┼──────────┼─────────────┤
│ Size         │ 12x8"   │ 15x10"  │ 18x12"  │ All 3 sizes │
│ Juice Groove │ No       │ Yes      │ Yes      │ Yes         │
│ Handles      │ No       │ No       │ Yes      │ Yes         │
│ Thickness    │ 0.5"     │ 0.75"    │ 1.0"     │ Mixed       │
│ Price        │ ₱1,000   │ ₱1,500   │ ₱2,000   │ ₱4,000      │

└──────────────┴──────────┴──────────┴──────────┴─────────────┘
```

**PPC benefit:** If a shopper clicks your ad for the Standard board but sees the comparison chart, they might buy the Large instead. Higher order value from the same click. That click just got more valuable.

### Module 2: Lifestyle Imagery

Full-bleed images showing the product in a real setting. These build purchase confidence.

**PPC benefit:** Shoppers who see lifestyle images are more likely to buy. A 10% improvement in conversion rate means your ACoS drops by roughly the same amount.

## Without Brand Registry: A Worked Example

The 45% and 30% ACoS figures below are hypothetical assumptions for this worked example, not a guaranteed effect of enrolling in Brand Registry. Real ACoS depends on your listing, category, and competition; use your own before/after data once you've enrolled, not this table's numbers.

| Month | Ad Sales | Ad Spend at 45% ACoS | Ad Spend at 30% ACoS | Modeled Spend Difference |
|-------|----------|----------------------|--------------------|---------|
| 1 | ₱20,000 | ₱9,000 | ₱6,000 | ₱3,000 |
| 3 | ₱50,000 | ₱22,500 | ₱15,000 | ₱7,500 |
| 6 | ₱100,000 | ₱45,000 | ₱30,000 | ₱15,000 |
| 12 | ₱200,000 | ₱90,000 | ₱60,000 | ₱30,000 |

If a listing genuinely moved from 45% to 30% ACoS, the gap at ₱200,000 in monthly ad sales would be **₱30,000 that month**. The modeled gap grows as sales grow. Brand Registry enrollment itself is free, and this modeled scenario is meant to show why a lower ACoS matters at scale, not to promise this exact result. The ability to run Sponsored Brands campaigns is a separate, concrete benefit of Brand Registry that many agencies expect as standard regardless of any ACoS change.

## How to Get Brand Registry

1. **Trademark your brand name.** This is the prerequisite. Can be registered or pending.
2. **Enroll at brandregistry.amazon.com.** Takes about 30 minutes with your trademark number.
3. **Set up A+ Content.** Available immediately after Brand Registry approval.
4. **Create your first A+ Content.** Start with the comparison chart and lifestyle modules.

> **Key Takeaways:**
> - **Brand Registry is free.** You only pay for the trademark (₱15k-₱30k one-time).
> - **A+ Content is estimated to improve conversion rate,** commonly cited at 5-17% (seller-reported) or up to 8-20% sales lift (Amazon's own framing); not a guaranteed figure, but directionally it lowers ACoS.
> - **Comparison charts are commonly cited as a top-converting A+ module** (not a formally verified ranking). Worth building for any product with multiple SKUs or sizes to compare.
> - **Brand Analytics** unlocks competitor keyword data, covered in Module 8.
> - **Without Brand Registry**, you can't run Sponsored Brands campaigns.
> - The **₱30k/year PPC savings** from A+ Content often pays for the trademark within months.
> - **Enroll now.** The approval process takes 1-2 weeks, so start while you build your campaigns.

## Your turn

Work this through before checking the answer.

Your client sells a ₱1,100 insulated lunch box. They're not Brand Registered yet (trademark filed but not approved), their CPC (cost per click) is ₱30, and their current conversion rate is 7%. Ad sales are running about ₱60,000 a month. The client asks whether spending ₱18,000 to complete their trademark this quarter is worth it before their Q4 push. Before reading further: what's the first thing to check before recommending that spend? And using this lesson's ACoS math, what happens to ACoS if A+ Content lifts conversion rate from 7% to roughly 10%?

**Work it through:** Check eligibility first. A pending trademark filed through Amazon's IP Accelerator program, or a pending application in some countries outside the US, can sometimes enroll in Brand Registry before full registration; a trademark filed directly with the US trademark office generally can't. Confirm the client's filing path and country before treating the ₱18,000 as a hard prerequisite. If full registration really is required: ACoS is ad spend divided by ad sales, which works out to CPC divided by (conversion rate times price). At a 7% conversion rate: ₱30 / (0.07 × ₱1,100) = ₱30 / ₱77, about 39% ACoS. If A+ Content lifts conversion rate to 10%, within this lesson's commonly cited range: ₱30 / (0.10 × ₱1,100) = ₱30 / ₱110, about 27% ACoS. Against ₱60,000 a month in ad sales, that roughly 12-point gap is worth about ₱7,000 a month in modeled spend saved, which would repay the ₱18,000 trademark cost in under 3 months, once the client's own before-and-after data confirms the lift actually happens. Tell the client: check eligibility first, and if the trademark really is required, it's worth finishing before Q4, treating the ACoS improvement as a hypothesis to confirm, not a guarantee.

## Listing audit rationale

Close the audit with a recommendation, not a link to Brand Registry. Record:

1. **Eligibility evidence:** Note the trademark status, filing path, marketplace, and whether the client can enroll now.
2. **Current baseline:** Record the current CPC, conversion rate, and ACoS.
3. **Recommendation:** Choose `enroll now`, `finish the trademark first`, or `improve the standard listing first`.
4. **Validation plan:** Name the before-and-after metric and review window you will use. Treat the projected lift as a hypothesis until the client's data confirms it.

For the lunch box, a defensible rationale is: "Confirm the filing path and Brand Registry eligibility before approving the ₱18,000 trademark cost. The current baseline is $0.60 CPC, 7% conversion rate, and about 39% ACoS. If enrollment requires the completed trademark, finish it before Q4, publish A+ Content, then compare conversion rate and ACoS against the pre-change baseline before promising any savings."

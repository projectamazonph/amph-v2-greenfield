## What you can do after this lesson

Explain the seed-to-ship keyword research workflow a real operator runs in production, name the four steps of the workflow, and read the simulator's scoring for keyword readiness before you commit a single peso to a campaign.

## Why this workflow exists

A campaign that goes live without a ranked keyword list burns spend on traffic that is unlikely to convert. The cheapest work in Amazon PPC is the work that happens before the campaign is on.

The Keyword Lab sim is the practice surface. The lesson is the workflow: seed the list from the listing and the offer, expand it with the data Amazon already gives you, score each term on intent and economics, dedupe to prevent internal competition, then group so the campaign matches.

## The decision in one sentence

Rank terms on intent and economics, not on volume. The hit list with explicit intent and target ACoS is what makes the campaign operable.

## What the workflow looks like end to end

A production operator who does this for real runs four steps, in order:

1. **Seed.** Pull the obvious terms from the product itself: the title, the bullets, the A+ content, the backend keywords, and the reviews. Each review phrase is a hint at how real shoppers describe the product.
2. **Expand.** Use Brand Analytics (if available), Helium 10 or similar tools, and the Amazon search suggestions. Add competitor ASIN targeting where it makes sense.
3. **Score.** Rank each term by intent (transactional vs browsing), by estimated CPC against your target ACoS, and by fit with the listing. A high-volume term that does not match the listing is a waste term.
4. **Dedupe and group.** Strip duplicate stems, normalize match types, and group by intent so each ad group has one purpose. A campaign with five intents will serve none of them well.

The output is a hit list with a column for term, intent, est CPC, est CVR, target ACoS, and the ad group it belongs to.

## Worked example

The workflow above is the walkthrough a real operator runs in production. Step through it once on the sim, name each step in your own words, and record the deliverable you would hand to the client.

## How to read the simulator's feedback

Keyword Lab scores you against four signals:

- **Seed coverage.** Did you cover the obvious terms from the listing and the offer?
- **Expansion depth.** Did you pull from sources beyond the listing (Brand Analytics, search suggestions, competitor ASINs)?
- **Scoring quality.** Did you rank terms on intent and economics, not just on volume?
- **Grouping.** Did you group by intent so each ad group has one purpose?

A high score with a vague intent column means you wrote a hit list that no campaign can use. A high score with explicit intent and economics means the next step is straightforward.

## What you can do after this lesson

After this lesson, you can check the likely cause of a bottom-of-funnel problem before pausing campaigns or doubling bids.

## The decision in one sentence

Check relevance, listing, offer, and product health in order, then choose the smallest lever that matches the evidence.

A campaign can receive clicks and still fail to create orders. It can also show high ACoS because CPC is too high, conversion is weak, or the search terms are wrong. When sales suddenly fall, the first cause may be outside the ad settings.

## Worked example

A product has 24 clicks, no orders, and several searches for a different material. The first action is to inspect the search terms and listing fit, then add only the clearly irrelevant negative. Do not double the bid because more traffic would add more waste.

## Quick check

Sales suddenly drop today. What should you check first?

**Answer:** Check stock, price, reviews, and competitors. A product or offer problem can look like an ad problem.

## Key Takeaways

- Separate product problems from traffic problems.
- Clicks without sales call for a relevance and listing check.
- High ACoS needs CPC, CVR, margin, and target context.
- A sudden sales drop is not permission to panic-pause the account.

## What you can do after this lesson

After this lesson, you can write a short client report that connects performance numbers to meaning and a proposed next action.

## The decision in one sentence

A useful report answers four questions in order: what happened, how it changed, what it means, and what happens next.

A client does not need every row in the export. They need a truthful readout they can act on. A VA in Iloilo can make the report easier to trust by showing the current number, the comparison, the interpretation, and the next check.

## Use the same five parts every week

Start with the core numbers: spend, sales, ACoS, clicks, orders, CTR, and conversion rate. Compare the current period with the prior period. Name what worked and what did not. End with one or two next steps, including the review date.

## Your turn

Write a four-sentence update for a campaign with ₱300 spend, ₱900 ad sales, 33% ACoS, and a 25% target. State the trend you still need to check, the likely problem, and one next action.

## Quick check

What should a report include after the numbers?

**Answer:** It should include the trend, what worked, what did not work, and the next steps. Numbers inform the client. A clear action makes the report useful.

## Evidence to record

Save the report with the date range, source export, owner, client question, and next review date. Keep the exact row or filter that supports each important claim.

## Key Takeaways

- Use a repeatable report shape.
- Compare with a prior period before naming a trend.
- Connect every important number to meaning.
- End with a small action and a review date.

## Retrieval cue

Can another person read your report and tell what to check next without asking you to explain it?
